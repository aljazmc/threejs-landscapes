# Bloodmoon Manor

A small first person shooter in Victorian gothic style.

| | Vampire hunter | Creatures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| health | 500 | 300 |
| moving speed | 4 m/s | 4 m/s |
| attack | revolver or steampunk flamethrower | blows, 50 damage, at most every 1.2 s |

| Weapon | Damage | Firing | Hits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 revolver (default) | 40 | a silver bullet every 0.5 s | only the first creature in the line of fire |
| 2 flamethrower | 150 per second | while the trigger is held and the tank has fuel: a full tank burns 4 s and refills in 8 s; once dry, it fires again at a quarter | every creature within 6 m in its cone of fire, not through walls |

| Mouse / key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| mouse | look around (click into the view to capture the mouse) |
| W A S D, arrow keys | move |
| Space, left click | fire (hold to keep firing) |
| 1, 2 | revolver, flamethrower |
| Esc | pause to the main menu |

On a phone or tablet the game goes fullscreen and sideways where the browser allows it (not on iPhones):

| Touch | Action |
| --- | --- |
| left half | a joystick where the thumb lands: walk in any direction, slower when tilted less |
| right half | drag to look around |
| FIRE button | fire (hold to keep firing) |
| the weapon panels | choose the revolver or the flamethrower |
| II at the top | pause to the main menu |

The main menu has *Play*, *Save*, *Load* and *Language* (English, Deutsch, Polski, Slovenščina); the saved game and the language are kept in the browser's local storage.

```
yarn s:bloodmoon    # dev server
yarn bp:bloodmoon   # production build into dist/, three.js from esm.sh
```

## Sources

`src/index.ts` starts `src/game/main.ts` (the letterboxed 960x540 view, the renderer, the screens, save and load). `menu.ts`, `play.ts` (controls and HUD), `game.ts` (the hunter and the weapons), `monster.ts`, `castle.ts` (map, hull, props, lights; collisions, rays, paths), `rig.ts` (models and their joints), `view.ts` (the creatures' models, the camera, the weapons in hand, the effects), `fx.ts`, `lights.ts`, `audio.ts`, `lang.ts`, `ui.ts` and `res.ts` (loading). The game's logic (`game.ts`, `monster.ts`, `fx.ts`) knows nothing of the page: controls go in, sounds come out, and the view draws it.

Map coordinates (z up) are three.js' (x, z, y), and the models keep their glTF PBR materials. The castle at night is lit with physical lights (a light of energy 1 is 450 lumens) and exposure, ACES tone mapping, bloom on the flames and glows, the reddish exponential fog, ambient light and a dim solid environment. three.js lights every pixel with every light, so `lights.ts` gives a pool of 16 point lights to the castle's lights that shine brightest at the camera. Texts are drawn in DejaVu Sans (`src/res/DejaVuSans.ttf`, with its license in `src/res/DejaVuSans-LICENSE.txt`).

## Resources

Everything in `src/res` is generated by the scripts beside `src`:

```
./project.sh graphics   # blender-scripts/ with Blender: the map, sprite sheets and GLB models
./project.sh audio      # fluidsynth-scripts/ with numpy and FluidSynth: sound effects and music (OGG, and for Safari WAV effects and MP3 music with ffmpeg)
```
